## Context

D-VMRP originally covered both the licence and the shape of a possible business. Liam accepted the licence part ("Closed"), so the business shape is split out here to be decided on its own.

## Decision

If InLoop becomes a product: the local CLI and MCP server, with local or bring-your-own-bucket session storage, are free. Hosted team features (instant board claims across people, a combined team view, notifications, a web viewer, team key management) are paid. Details in docs/09.

## Consequences

No business features are built until dogfooding proves the tool.
